JSON Schema (draft-07) cho từng collection, gồm:

Kiểu dữ liệu, ràng buộc bắt buộc, enum cho section (A.I, A.II, A.III, B.I, B.II), task_type (ke_hoach, dot_xuat, thuong_xuyen…), status.

Các trường đề xuất:

employees: employee_code, full_name, department, position, email.
reports: employee_id, period_from, period_to, report_month_key (yyyyMM), created_at.
tasks: report_id, section, stt, title, task_type, time_from, time_to, frequency, result, difficulty_note, cost_vnd, project_id?, parent_task_id?, tags[].
subtasks: giống tasks nhưng có parent_task_id bắt buộc.
evaluations: report_id, topic (hang_ngay, chuyen_mon), content.
projects: project_code, project_name, owner_dept.
attachments: report_id/task_id, file_name, mime_type, source_path.
